3.1 ONE-TIME REGISTRATION VIA WHATSAPP-LINKED MOBILE NUMBER
======================================================================

As a citizen, I want to register on Jan-Setu using my WhatsApp-linked mobile number, so that I can access both the web app and WhatsApp bot under a single identity.

Specific:
OTP sent to WhatsApp-linked mobile; one account grants access to both channels.

Measurable:
OTP delivered within 60 sec; registration completed under 2 min.

Actionable:
OTP verification via WhatsApp Business API.

Relevant:
Removes first-time digital entry barrier — P4, P6.

Time-bound:
Full registration in under 2 minutes.

Acceptance Criteria:
• OTP delivered within 60 seconds; registration completes in under 2 minutes.
• One account grants access to both web app and WhatsApp bot.
• UI available in Hindi and English.

Reduces duplicate complaint volume — P9, P10.
